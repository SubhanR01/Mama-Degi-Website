01 · The degi

Cooked in the degi.
Served on brass.

A degi is the heavy, round-bellied pot our chicken is slow-cooked in. The chicken is roasted until the spices darken and the edges char, then laid over saffron biryani on a hammered brass tray.

مرغ دیگیDegi chickenWhole chicken, deep-spiced and roasted until the skin crackles.
بریانیBiryani riceLong grains, half saffron gold, half white, cooked to stay separate.
سالاتFresh saladCrisp lettuce, onion rings, olives and lemon to cut the richness.
